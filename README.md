# Growth Audit — Lingopie

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/lingopie-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Lingopie already has, plus a clickable prototype of the iOS onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Lingopie's own design. Only the order of the screens changed.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask what they binge before the price, and let the paywall name the show they picked | What you binge → Pick a show → Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Offer the 7-day free trial from lingopie.com when someone closes the paywall | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 03 | Ask for the account after they pay, not right before the price | Account | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 04 | Put 2M+ learners, 4.4 on the App Store and 4.8 on Trustpilot on the paywall | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Ask for notifications after the paywall | Notifications | CR +10–15% |

## Where the facts come from

Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from Lingopie's own published material: the in-app screens (99,99 € a year, 14,99 € a month, Save 44%, the four paywall lines), the App Store listing (4.4 from 2.2K ratings, 8 free minutes a week), lingopie.com (2M+ learners, 4.8 on Trustpilot) and the pricing page (7 days free on every plan).

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Lingopie.
